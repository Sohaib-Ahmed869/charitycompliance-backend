# Checklist Module Mapping Guide

This guide defines sidebar modules/submodules, purpose, and keyword hints used for checklist remapping.

## Governance

- Path: `GOVERNANCE > Charity Administration > Registrations & Licenses`
- What it does: Registration, licences, permits, renewals, expiry tracking, issuing authorities.
- Keywords: registration, licence, license, permit, renewal, expiry, issuing authority, ACNC registration, ABN

- Path: `GOVERNANCE > Charity Administration > Responsible People`
- What it does: Board/director/responsible-person governance and fit-proper checks.
- Keywords: responsible person, board member, director, trustee, police check, WWCC, fit and proper, consent

- Path: `GOVERNANCE > Charity Administration > Governing Doc`
- What it does: Constitution, trust deed, governing rules and related document governance.
- Keywords: constitution, trust deed, governing document, by-law, charter, governing rules

- Path: `GOVERNANCE > Charity Administration > Approval Thresholds`
- What it does: Delegations and financial/approval authority limits.
- Keywords: approval threshold, delegation, authority limit, approval limit, signatory

- Path: `GOVERNANCE > Charity Administration > Yearly Statements`
- What it does: Annual statements and annual regulatory reporting artifacts.
- Keywords: annual statement, annual report, yearly statement, AIS, ACNC annual

- Path: `GOVERNANCE > Policies & Procedures`
- What it does: Policy/procedure lifecycle, review, versioning and distribution.
- Keywords: policy, procedure, review date, version, policy acknowledgement

- Path: `GOVERNANCE > Conflict of Interest`
- What it does: COI declarations, disclosures, conflicts and mitigation.
- Keywords: conflict of interest, COI, declaration of interest, disclosure
